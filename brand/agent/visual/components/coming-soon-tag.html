coming-soon-tag

Studio is the one thing that genuinely isn't out yet. This component's whole job is to say so in a way that cannot be misread as availability.

Use it when

  • Naming Frameless Studio, anywhere.
  • Section 8 of the mandatory page composition.
  • You want the reader to know something is intended without thinking they can have it.

Don't use it when

  • The thing is available. That spends the credibility the brand runs on.
  • You were going to add a countdown, a waitlist, or "be the first".
  • You were going to add a CTA. There is nothing to sign up for yet.

callout

Where the page says the exact thing — the limit, the number, the part that is not true. A field guide that refuses to claim an unconfirmed sighting is the one you trust.

Use it when

  • Stating a real constraint, including one that is inconvenient.
  • Giving a number with where it came from.
  • Warning about a way to get it genuinely wrong.

Don't use it when

  • You want emphasis. That is what weight and space are for.
  • The sentence would be just as true in a paragraph.
  • You already used three on this page. Then none of them reads as important.
